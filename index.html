Cabin workshop — meditation & taiji rooting qigong

Rooted Wild Reset

Root yourself somewhere real. This cabin bootcamp is built on stillness, rooting, and breath — taught outdoors, under the trees, whenever the sky allows it. Just a 2-minute walk to the National Forest, where you'll train and reset the mind and body.

Each session pairs seated meditation with taiji rooting qigong foundation work — the practice of sinking weight, finding your center, and moving from ground up. We train outside as much as the weather lets us: forest floor instead of studio floor, wind instead of white noise.

What does training look like?

A four-part foundation
Meditation Based on the Navigating the Mind framework. Whether you're a beginner or advanced, you'll learn new ways to observe the mind.
Awaken the Dragon Qigong Learn the 13 postures for rooting and build toward being able to stand for a full hour — you'll get the method and the experience of how to get there.
Taiji Quan training The foundation of energy, rooting, and movement that applies across all taiji forms.
Wing Chun Chi Sao training For Wing Chun instructors or students looking to improve their rooting in Wing Chun — we can train specific rooting & energy work for Wing Chun.

You can customize and choose what you'd like to train more on. If meditation is all you want, we can make the whole session meditation training only.

Your teacher

Choying Huynh

Choying teaches an integrated system of Taiji Quan, Qigong, Wing Chun, and meditation — practices rooted in traditional Chinese internal arts and built around one idea: the body, breath, and mind aren't separate things to fix, they're one system to know.

He trains under Next Level Taiji in Denver, who trained directly under Master Chen ZiQiang, the 20th-generation disciple of Chenjiagou Chen-style Tai Chi — including Lao Jia Yi Lu (Old Frame First Road), the foundational parent form of Chen-style practice. His Wing Chun training comes from the Ip Ching lineage in both St. Louis and Denver, with 15+ years of Wing Chun Kung Fu, developing both the internal and external sides of the art.

Rather than teaching Taiji, meditation, Qigong, and Wing Chun as separate subjects, Choying built a single framework with three entry points — movement for those who need to move to think, stillness for those who need to stop and feel, and Qigong for those who need something tangible to believe. Out of that framework came Awaken the Dragon Within, his 13-posture standing Qigong system distilled to root a practitioner's foundation without vague instruction or guesswork.

He's the founder of Simple Mind Simple Movement and of Integration Meditation, LLC (2017) — creator of the Navigating The Mind framework, a foundation for breaking free from programming — a Denver and Edwardsville-based meditation space built around a 4-week workshop progression — observing the mind, breaking patterns, reprogramming, and integration. Across both, the throughline is the same: direct experience over dogma, structure you can feel in your own body, and practice built to compound over a lifetime.

House rules & boundaries

Please read before booking

This is a private residence — not a commercial retreat center. You're a guest in someone's home, on someone's land, in someone's neighborhood. A few things we ask of everyone who stays or trains here:

  • This isn't a place to stay like Airbnb — it's designed for training the mind and body.
  • Reach out first before you book — a 15-min call to make sure we're aligned on what you're looking for and why you want to come here to learn.
  • Respect the cabin and property as you would a friend's home — leave it as you found it.
  • Quiet hours and noise levels are kept low out of respect for neighbors.
  • Stay within the marked training areas and trails; the surrounding land is private and/or shared with neighboring residents.
  • No smoking indoors, and any open flame or fire follows local guidelines.
  • Alcohol and substances are not part of this practice space.
  • No outside pets, please — we already have Bodhi, our blue heeler, keeping us company on the land.
  • Cancellations and property damage are handled case by case — reach out directly with any questions before booking.
  • No laundry service at the cabin — if you want to do laundry, there's a laundromat 35 minutes into town.
  • No A/C — we run cooler than town thanks to plenty of tree cover and shade. Here's the average weather from nearby Woodland Park:
    • June: highs ~72°F, lows ~45°F
    • July: highs ~75°F, lows ~51°F
    • August: highs ~71°F, lows ~44°F
  • We have a holding tank, not a septic system — it costs to pump when full, so please be mindful of water usage.
  • Food isn't part of this bootcamp, but you're welcome to cook — there's a full kitchen to prep a meal, or we can cook together. If there's something you need, let us know and we'll have it ready when you arrive.
  • If you don't align or agree with any of these rules, this place isn't for you. You're still welcome to come train — just seek out a hostel in town, about 35 minutes from here.

Getting here: airport, arrival & departure

  • Airfare and transportation are not included — you'll need to arrange your own way here.
  • The nearest airport is Colorado Springs, about 1 hour away — depending on traffic it can be up to 1.5 hours.
  • It's a dirt road with easy access, but if you're coming in winter, a 4x4 or AWD vehicle is required.

These boundaries exist so this space can stay open, personal, and sustainable — thank you for honoring them.
